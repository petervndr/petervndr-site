## Pre-Written Expert Quotes (Ready to Drop Into Responses)

### On marketing for professional services:
"Most professional service firms sell the solution instead of the problem. They lead with 'we offer tax planning' when they should lead with 'your tax bill is $40K higher than it needs to be.' Whoever demonstrates the clearest understanding of the problem gets the privilege of solving it."

### On referral dependency:
"Referrals are a golden leash. They feel great — warm leads, high close rates. But you have zero control over volume, timing, or quality. The moment you want to grow faster than your network can feed you, you hit a wall."

### On niche marketing:
"When I niched down to only serving accounting firms, my close rate went up, my pricing went up, and my marketing costs went down. Specificity is leverage. I helped a bookkeeper for moving companies go from $30K to $90K+ in monthly recurring revenue in 12 months because we spoke directly to one audience."

### On marketing ROI for small businesses:
"One of my clients, a real estate CPA, built a VSL funnel where we could put a dollar in and get six dollars out. That's when everything changed — he went from $100K to $3M in three years. But the funnel didn't work until we nailed the messaging. Most businesses skip that step."

### On the state of marketing agencies:
"Most accounting firms have been burned by a marketing agency at least once. They spent $40K and got nothing. The problem isn't usually the tactics — it's that the agency didn't understand the business model deeply enough to build messaging that actually resonates with the right prospects."
